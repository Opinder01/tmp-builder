Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This application ("the App") is a private, internal dispatch and timesheet system built and operated by Crown Traffic Management Ltd. for the sole use of its own employees, contractors, and administrators. It is not available to the public and does not offer any public sign-up.

Information we collect

The App collects and stores the following information, entered by administrators or workers:

  • Name, email address, and phone number of employees and contractors
  • Job dispatch details (job number, location, scheduled time, notes)
  • Submitted timesheets, including typed start/end times and a photo of the worker's paper timesheet slip
  • Wage, billing rate, and hours information used for payroll and invoicing

How we use this information

Information collected is used exclusively to operate Crown Traffic Management Ltd.'s internal dispatch, timesheet-approval, payroll-hours, and customer-invoicing workflow. Approved timesheet hours are shared with our QuickBooks Online account for the sole purpose of recording payroll time entries and generating invoices/bills. We do not sell, rent, or share this information with any other third party.

Data storage and security

Data is stored in a private, access-controlled database. Timesheet photos and attachments are stored in private cloud storage and are only accessible to authorized administrators and the worker who submitted them. Access to the App requires an account created by an administrator — there is no public registration.

Data retention

Dispatch and timesheet records are retained for as long as necessary for payroll, invoicing, and business record-keeping purposes.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to Crown Traffic Management Ltd. at info@crowntraffic.ca.
